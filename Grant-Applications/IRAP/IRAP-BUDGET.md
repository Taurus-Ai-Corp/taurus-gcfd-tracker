## No Double-Dipping Note

Costs claimed under IRAP **cannot** also be claimed under SR&ED. The strategy:

- **IRAP covers:** Lead developer salary during the 10-month project + 50% of contractor costs
- **SR&ED covers:** All other qualifying R&D expenditures NOT funded by IRAP (e.g., R&D work done before IRAP starts, work by team members not on the IRAP project, overhead via proxy method)

This is compliant with CRA/NRC stacking rules. The ITA will confirm the boundary during assessment.
