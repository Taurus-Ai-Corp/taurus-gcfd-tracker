| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Grant range | CAD 20,000–1,000,000/year |
| Duration | 1–5 years |
| Funding model | NSERC matches industry contributions (2:1 ratio typical) |

---

## Why NSERC Alliance

NSERC Alliance is the most valuable long-term funding program for us because:

1. **2:1 match ratio** — For every dollar TAURUS contributes, NSERC contributes $2. Our $50K becomes $150K.
2. **Multi-year funding** — Up to 5 years of sustained R&D support
3. **Talent pipeline** — Funds HQP (Highly Qualified Personnel) — grad students and postdocs who can join TAURUS
4. **Publication and credibility** — University partnership validates our technology in the academic community
5. **No equity dilution** — Pure grant funding with no ownership implications

## Research Components for Alliance Grant

### Which R&D Maps to an Alliance Grant

| Research Area | TAURUS Need | University Contribution | Fit |
|---------------|-------------|------------------------|-----|
| **Novel coherence metrics** (beyond PLV) | Phase-Amplitude Coupling (PAC), Modulation Index, cross-frequency directionality | Methodology development, statistical framework | Excellent |
| **Clinical validation studies** | Validate presets against clinical populations | Access to clinical EEG datasets, IRB approval, patient cohorts | Excellent |
| **Neurofeedback protocol development** | Define closed-loop protocols using our streaming API | Protocol design, efficacy studies, randomized controlled trials | Good |
| **Quantum biology foundations** | Theoretical basis for Posner molecule coherence claims | Physics/chemistry modeling, experimental validation | Good (long-term) |
| **Hardware integration** | OpenBCI, Muse, clinical EEG compatibility | Hardware testing, signal quality assessment | Moderate |

### Priority Research Project: Clinical Validation of Cross-Frequency Coherence Presets

**Title:** Systematic Validation of Automated EEG Cross-Frequency Coherence Analysis for Neurological Condition Classification

**Abstract:** This project will validate the taurus-gcfd-tracker's clinical preset system against multi-site clinical EEG data from patients with MDD, MCI, epilepsy, and ADHD. The university partner will provide access to clinical EEG databases and domain expertise in biomarker validation. TAURUS AI Corp will provide the analysis software, computational infrastructure, and engineering support. The goal is to publish validation results in a peer-reviewed journal, establishing the first open-source, clinically validated EEG cross-frequency coherence toolkit.

## University PI Requirements

### Must-Have Criteria

| Criterion | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| Faculty at a Canadian university | NSERC eligibility requirement |
| Active NSERC Discovery Grant | Demonstrates NSERC track record (strongly preferred) |
| Published work involving EEG cross-frequency coupling or PLV | Direct relevance to our technology |
| Access to clinical EEG datasets | Required for validation studies |
| Willingness to supervise HQP on this project | Alliance grants fund students/postdocs |

### Nice-to-Have Criteria

| Criterion | Benefit |
|-----------|---------|
| Clinical collaborators (psychiatrists, neurologists) | Access to patient cohorts for prospective validation |
| IRB approval for EEG studies | Faster path to clinical data collection |
| Experience with NSERC Alliance applications | Improves application quality and success odds |
| Interest in open-source neuroscience tools | Alignment with TAURUS open-core philosophy |

### Target PI Search Strategy

1. Search PubMed for recent papers on "theta-gamma coupling" + "EEG" + "Canada"
2. Cross-reference with NSERC grant database (nserc-crsng.gc.ca) for active Discovery Grant holders
3. Check faculty pages for current grad students (indicates capacity to supervise)
4. Prioritize PIs who have published using MNE-Python (aligned with our ecosystem)

## Stacking Considerations

| Program | Stacking with NSERC Alliance |
|---------|------------------------------|
| IRAP | Compatible — different expenditures (IRAP: internal labour; NSERC: university research) |
| SR&ED | TAURUS's cash contribution to NSERC Alliance is NOT SR&ED eligible (it's a payment to a university, not a direct R&D expenditure). However, in-kind contributions (developer time on joint research) may qualify. |
| Mitacs | Compatible and complementary — Mitacs funds the intern, NSERC Alliance funds the broader research program. Same PI can supervise both. |
