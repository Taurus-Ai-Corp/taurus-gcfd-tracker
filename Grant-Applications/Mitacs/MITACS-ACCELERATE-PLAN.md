## Internship Scope

### Project: Clinical EEG Coherence Validation and Benchmark Development

The intern will work on tasks that directly support the GCFD Coherence Tracker's v2.0 release, focusing on areas where neuroscience domain expertise is critical:

| Task | Duration | Deliverable |
|------|----------|------------|
| Literature review: compile clinical theta-gamma coherence values | 3 weeks | Structured database of 30+ studies with methods, values, hardware |
| Dataset curation: identify and prepare public EEG datasets for each condition | 2 weeks | Preprocessed datasets from PhysioNet, TDBRAIN, OpenNeuro |
| Preset validation: run coherence pipeline on clinical datasets | 4 weeks | Validation report comparing our output to published values |
| Benchmark comparison: taurus-gcfd vs MNE-Python vs EEGLAB | 3 weeks | Published benchmark with statistical analysis |
| Documentation: write clinical user guide and methodology paper draft | 2 weeks | User guide + draft manuscript |
| Presentation: present findings at lab meeting or conference | 1 week | Poster or talk |

**Total: ~15 weeks (4 months)**

### Why This Needs a Neuroscience Trainee

This work requires:
- Understanding of EEG preprocessing conventions across different clinical contexts
- Ability to interpret published methods sections and extract comparable parameters
- Knowledge of clinical condition characteristics (what MDD EEG looks like vs. MCI vs. epilepsy)
- Statistical training for biomarker validation

A software engineer cannot do this work effectively. It requires domain expertise at the MSc/PhD level.

## Stacking with IRAP

Mitacs intern costs are **separate** from IRAP-claimed labour — the intern is not the lead developer or contractor listed in the IRAP budget. No double-dipping concern:

- IRAP covers: founder salary + contractor salary
- Mitacs covers: intern stipend
- SR&ED can cover: the company's $7,500 Mitacs contribution (it's a qualifying R&D expenditure)
